Hanzi Burst — third-party data and licenses

The offline dictionary data bundled in data/ is derived from the following sources.

1. Make Me a Hanzi — https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
   Character decompositions, definitions, pinyin, etymology (dictionary.txt).
   Licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL) v3,
   https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html . Stroke data derives from
   Arphic PL KaiTi / Arphic PL UKai, under the Arphic Public License.
   (Stroke outlines are not bundled in this extension.)

2. CC-CEDICT — https://cc-cedict.org/
   Chinese-English word dictionary. Copyright MDBG and CC-CEDICT contributors,
   licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
   Entries were reformatted and split into per-character-range JSON shards.
   The resulting data files are distributed under the same CC BY-SA license.

3. Unicode / Unihan — https://www.unicode.org/
   Radical names and fallback definitions, under the Unicode License,
   https://www.unicode.org/license.txt .

4. JMdict — https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
   Japanese word dictionary (English and Russian glosses) used to split Japanese
   text into words (data/ja/) and for Russian kanji meanings (data/kanji/).
   Copyright the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG),
   licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Converted from
   jmdict-simplified, https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified .
   See https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html .

5. KANJIDIC2 — https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project
   On and kun readings and English meanings of kanji (data/kanji/).
   Copyright EDRDG, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .

6. KanjiVG — https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/
   Breakdown of kanji into components (data/kanji/). Copyright Ulrich Apel,
   licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ .
   (Stroke outlines are not bundled in this extension.)

7. cjkvi-ids — https://github.com/cjkvi/cjkvi-ids
   Ideographic Description Sequences, used only to fill in breakdowns of
   characters that Make Me a Hanzi does not cover. Copyright CJKVI Database,
   based on the CHISE IDS Database; licensed under the GNU General Public
   License v2, https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html .

8. complete-hsk-vocabulary — https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
   HSK 3.0 levels of words and characters. Copyright Dr.Kameleon,
   licensed under the MIT License, https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT .

The data files derived from sources 4-6 are distributed under the same CC BY-SA licenses.

Russian-language definitions are original translations by the Hanzi Burst project.
